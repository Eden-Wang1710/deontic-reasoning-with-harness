For purposes of this section-

    (3) Phaseout

amount shall be reduced by the applicable percentage.

        (B) Applicable percentage

        For purposes of subparagraph (A), the term "applicable percentage" means 2 percentage points for each $2,500 (or fraction thereof) by which the taxpayer
