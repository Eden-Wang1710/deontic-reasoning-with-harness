Alice is an unmarried individual filing as a single taxpayer under §1(c). Her gross income is $311,510.

For standard deduction, §63(c)(2)(C) provides $3,000 for unmarried individuals not falling under other categories.

For personal exemptions, §151 provides:
- $2,000 for the taxpayer (Alice) under §151(b)
- $2,000 for Bob as a dependent under §151(c)

- Relationship test met: Bob is Alice's father (§152(d)(2)(C))
