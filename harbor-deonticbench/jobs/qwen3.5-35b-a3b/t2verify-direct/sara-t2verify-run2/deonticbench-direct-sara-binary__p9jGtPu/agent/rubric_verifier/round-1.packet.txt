## Statute

Section 2(b)(1)(B) (lines 220-221):
"(B) maintains a household which constitutes for such taxable year the principal place of abode of the father or mother of the taxpayer, if the taxpayer is entitled to a deduction for the taxable year for such father or mother under section 151."

## Reasoning

The question asks whether section 2(b)(1)(B) applies to Bob in 2018.

Section 2(b)(1)(B) requires that the taxpayer maintains a household for the principal place of abode of the taxpayer's "father or mother" and is entitled to a deduction for such father or mother under section 151.

The case facts state:
- Charlie is Bob's child, born October 9th, 2000
- Bob is entitled to a deduction for Charlie under section 151(c) for the years 2015 to 2019

Charlie is Bob's son, not his father or mother. Therefore, section 2(b)(1)(B) does not apply to Bob in 2018.

The claim that "Section 2(b)(1)(B) applies to Bob in 2018" is false because Charlie (the dependent for whom Bob claims a deduction) is Bob's son, not his father or mother as required by section 2(b)(1)(B).

## Answer
Answer: Contradiction
