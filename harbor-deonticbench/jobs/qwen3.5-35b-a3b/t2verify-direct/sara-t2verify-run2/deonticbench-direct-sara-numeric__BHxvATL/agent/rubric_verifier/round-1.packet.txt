3. **Standard Deduction**: Under §153(b)(2)(B), the basic standard deduction for a head of household is $4,400.

4. **Personal Exemptions**: Under §151(b), Alice is entitled to an exemption of the exemption amount for herself. Under §151(c), Alice is entitled to an exemption for Bob as her dependent. Under §151(d)(1), the exemption amount is $2,000. Thus, total exemptions = $2,000 (Alice) + $2,000 (Bob) = $4,000.

5. **Taxable Income**: Computed as gross income minus standard deduction minus personal exemptions:

   ```
   Taxable Income = $22,895 - $4,400 - $4,000 = $14,495
   ```

6. **Tax Calculation**: Under §1(b), the tax for a head of household is 15% of taxable income if the taxable income is not over $29,600. Since $14,495 is not over $29,600:
