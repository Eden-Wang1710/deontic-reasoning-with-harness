"For purposes of paragraph (1)(A), an individual bears a relationship to the taxpayer described in this paragraph if the individual is any of the following with respect to the taxpayer:

5. For the relationship in 152(d)(2)(F) to apply, Bob would need to be "a brother or sister of the father or mother of Alice" - meaning Bob would need to be a sibling of Alice's parent.

6. Bob is Charlie's son, not a sibling of Alice's parent. There is no evidence in the facts that Bob is a brother or sister of Alice's father or mother.

7. The relationship described in 152(d)(2)(F) does not apply because Bob is not a brother or sister of Alice's parent.
