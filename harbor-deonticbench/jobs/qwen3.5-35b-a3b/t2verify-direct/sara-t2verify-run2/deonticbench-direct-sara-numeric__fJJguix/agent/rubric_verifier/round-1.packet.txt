**Step 1: Determine Alice's filing status**

Alice is unmarried (no spouse is mentioned in the facts). Under §2(b), Alice qualifies as head of household because:
- She is not married at the close of her taxable year
- She maintains a household (the house she shares with Bob) as the principal place of abode for her father Bob for more than one-half of the taxable year (she has lived there since 2007)
- She furnishes over half of the household costs (75%)
- Bob is her dependent (see Step 2)

**Step 2: Determine if Bob qualifies as a dependent**

Under §152(d), Bob qualifies as a qualifying relative because:
- §152(d)(2)(C): Bob is Alice's father (qualifying relationship)
- The facts do not mention Bob having any income, so we assume he has no income as required by §152(d)(1)(B)
- Bob is not a qualifying child (he is older than 25 years old)

**Step 3: Calculate personal exemptions**

Under §151(b) and §151(c), Alice gets:
- 1 exemption for herself = $2,000 (§151(d))
- 1 exemption for Bob (dependent) = $2,000 (§151(c))
- Total exemptions = $4,000

**Step 4: Check exemption phaseout**

Under §151(d)(3), the exemption phaseout applies only if AGI exceeds the applicable amount under §68.

Under §68(b)(1)(C), the applicable amount for an unmarried individual is $250,000.

Alice's AGI = $67,285 < $250,000, so no phaseout applies.

**Step 5: Calculate taxable income**

Alice itemizes deductions ($17,817 is given). Under §150(b), taxable income = AGI - itemized deductions - personal exemptions.

Taxable income = $67,285 - $17,817 - $4,000 = $45,468

**Step 6: Calculate tax**

Under §1(b)(ii) for head of households:
- Taxable income of $45,468 is over $29,600 but not over $76,400
- Tax = $4,440 + 28% of excess over $29,600
- Excess = $45,468 - $29,600 = $15,868
- Tax = $4,440 + 0.28 × $15,868 = $4,440 + $4,443.04 = $8,883.04
