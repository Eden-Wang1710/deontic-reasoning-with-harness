(1) In general

## Reasoning

The statute §152(b)(1) states: "If an individual is a dependent of a taxpayer for any taxable year of such taxpayer beginning in a calendar year, such individual shall be treated as having no dependents for any taxable year of such individual beginning in such calendar year."

For §152(b)(1) to apply to Alice, Alice must be a dependent of a taxpayer. The statute only applies to individuals who are themselves dependents.

The case facts state:
- Bob satisfies §152(c)(1) for 2015 (Bob is Alice's qualifying child/dependent)
- Charlie satisfies §152(c)(1) for 2015 (Charlie is Bob's qualifying child/dependent)
- Alice's income in 2015 was $504,598
- Bob had no income in 2015

The facts indicate that Bob is Alice's dependent (qualifying child), and Charlie is Bob's dependent (qualifying child). However, there is NO fact indicating that Alice is anyone's dependent. Alice is the taxpayer who claims Bob as her dependent.

Since Alice is not a dependent of any taxpayer, §152(b)(1) does not apply to her. The claim that "Section 152(b)(1) applies to Alice for the year 2015" is false.
