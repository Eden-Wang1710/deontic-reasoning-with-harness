## Reasoning

**Step 1: Determine filing status**
Alice's spouse Bob died October 22, 2016. For the 2017 taxable year, Bob's death occurred within the two years immediately preceding 2017 (§2(a)(1)(A)). Alice maintains a household with Charlie, her son, who is a dependent (§2(a)(1)(B)). Therefore, Alice qualifies as a surviving spouse under §2(a)(1).

**Step 2: Verify Charlie is a qualifying child dependent**
Charlie is Alice's son (§152(c)(2)(A)), has the same principal place of abode as Alice (§152(c)(1)(B)), and is less than 25 years old at the end of 2017 (born October 4, 2004, so 12 years old) (§152(c)(3)). Charlie qualifies as a qualifying child under §152(c).

**Step 3: Determine deductions**
Alice's gross income for 2017 was $567,192. She donated $59,850 to charity.

Standard deduction for surviving spouse: 200% × $3,000 = $6,000 (§162(c)(2)(A), §162(c)(2)(C)).

Since itemized deduction ($59,850) > standard deduction ($6,000), Alice itemizes.

**Step 4: Apply §68 reduction**
§68(f) states §68 does NOT apply to years beginning AFTER December 31, 2017. Since 2017 begins on January 1, 2017 (not after December 31, 2017), §68 DOES apply.

Under §68(a), itemized deductions are reduced by the lesser of:
- 3% of AGI excess over applicable amount (§68(b)(1)(A) = $300,000 for surviving spouse)
- 80% of otherwise allowable deductions

AGI before §68 reduction = $567,192 - $59,850 = $507,342
Excess over $300,000 = $507,342 - $300,000 = $207,342
3% of excess = 0.03 × $207,342 = $6,220.26
80% of deductions = 0.80 × $59,850 = $47,880

Reduction = min($6,220.26, $47,880) = $6,220.26
Allowed itemized deduction = $59,850 - $6,220.26 = $53,629.74

AGI = $567,192 - $53,629.74 = $513,562.26

**Step 5: Personal exemption phaseout**
Exemption amount = $2,000 (§151(d)(1)). This is NOT zero because §151(d)(5) only applies to years beginning AFTER December 31, 2017.

Number of exemptions: Alice + Charlie = 2 (§151(b), §151(c))

Phaseout (§151(d)(3)(B)): AGI ($513,562.26) exceeds applicable amount ($300,000) by $213,562.26.
Fraction of $2,500 = $213,562.26 / $2,500 = 85.424904
Rounded up to 86 fractions ("or fraction thereof")
Phaseout percentage = 86 × 2% = 172%, capped at 100% (§151(d)(3)(B))

Since phaseout is 100%, total exemptions = $0.

**Step 6: Taxable income**
Taxable income = AGI - exemptions = $513,562.26 - $0 = $513,562.26

**Step 7: Calculate tax**
Alice's taxable income ($513,562.26) exceeds $250,000, so §1(a)(1)(v) applies:
Tax = $75,528.50 + 39.6% × ($513,562.26 - $250,000)
Tax = $75,528.50 + 0.396 × $263,562.26
Tax = $75,528.50 + $104,370.65496
Tax = $179,899.15496

Rounded to whole number: $179,899
