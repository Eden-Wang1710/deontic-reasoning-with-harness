"(B) unless, for the taxpayer's taxable year during which his spouse died, a joint return could have been made. A husband and wife may make a single return jointly of income taxes, even though one of the spouses has neither gross income nor deductions, except that no joint return shall be made if either the husband or wife at any time during the taxable year is a nonresident alien."

## Reasoning

The facts state:
- Alice died on July 9th, 2014 (taxable year = 2014)
- Alice was a nonresident alien since March 4th, 1990 (meaning she was a nonresident alien throughout 2014)

Section 2(a)(2)(B) provides that a taxpayer shall not be considered a surviving spouse unless a joint return could have been made for the taxable year during which the spouse died. The section further states that "no joint return shall be made if either the husband or wife at any time during the taxable year is a nonresident alien."

Since Alice was a nonresident alien at all times during 2014 (she became a nonresident alien on March 4th, 1990), no joint return could have been made for 2014.

Therefore, Section 2(a)(2)(B) applies to Bob in 2014, preventing him from being considered a surviving spouse.
