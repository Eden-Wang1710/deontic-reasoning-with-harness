## Reasoning

1. Filing Status: Alice was legally separated under a decree of divorce on March 2nd, 2015 (§7703(a)(2), line 510). Therefore, she is "not married" for tax purposes in 2017.

   - Charlie is her qualifying child
   - Charlie is her child (§152(c)(2)(A), line 119)
   - Charlie has the same principal place of abode as Alice for more than half the year ("Alice and Charlie live in a house maintained by Alice")
   - Charlie is 5 years old in 2017 (born Feb 2, 2012), meeting the age requirement of <25 years (§152(c)(3), line 127)
   - Charlie has not filed a joint return

3. Standard Deduction: As a head of household, Alice's basic standard deduction is $4,400 (§63(c)(2)(B), line 394).

4. Personal Exemptions: Alice gets:
   - $2,000 for herself (§151(d)(1), line 70)
   - $2,000 for Charlie as a dependent (§151(c), line 64; §151(d)(1), line 70)
   - Total exemptions: $4,000

5. Taxable Income Computation:
   - Gross income: $9,560 (given fact)
   - Less standard deduction: $4,400 (§63(c)(2)(B), line 394)
   - Less exemptions: $4,000 (§151(c), line 64; §151(d)(1), line 70)
   - Taxable income = $9,560 - $4,400 - $4,000 = $1,160

6. Tax Calculation:
   - Taxable income of $1,160 is not over $29,600
   - Tax rate: 15% (§1(b)(i), line 23)
   - Tax = 15% × $1,160 = $174
