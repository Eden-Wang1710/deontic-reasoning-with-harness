## Reasoning

   - Emily: 10 occasions × $550 = $5,500
   - Fred: 9 occasions × $550 = $4,950
   - George: 8 occasions × $550 = $4,400
   - Total: $31,350 (computed with python3)

3. Under Section 269, Alice is an employer because she paid wages of $20,000 or more ($31,350 > $20,000).

4. Under Section 247, Alice owes an excise tax of 6% of total wages: 0.06 × $31,350 = $1,881.

5. Taxable income calculation:
   - Gross income: $567,192 (given fact)
   - Less standard deduction: $3,000 (Section 395(C) for unmarried)
   - Less personal exemption: $2,000 (Section 72)
   - Taxable income: $567,192 - $3,000 - $2,000 = $562,192

6. Income tax (Section 37, unmarried over $250,000):
   - Base tax: $79,772

   - Additional tax: 0.396 × $312,192 = $123,628.032
   - Total income tax: $79,772 + $123,628.032 = $203,400.032

7. Total tax = Income tax + Employer excise tax
   - Total tax = $203,400.032 + $1,881 = $205,281.032
   - Rounded to whole number: $205,281
