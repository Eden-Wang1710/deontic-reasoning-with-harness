1. **Filing Status**: Bob is unmarried (no spouse mentioned in the facts). Bob maintains a household that constitutes the principal place of abode of his son Charlie for more than one-half of 2015. Charlie has not filed a joint return (§152(c)(1)(E)). Therefore, Bob qualifies as Head of Household under §2(b)(1)(A)(i).

2. **Personal Exemptions**: Bob is entitled to a personal exemption of $2,000 for himself under §151(b). Bob is also entitled to a personal exemption of $2,000 for Charlie as a dependent under §151(c). Total personal exemptions = $2,000 + $2,000 = $4,000.

3. **Standard Deduction**: As a Head of Household who takes the standard deduction, Bob is entitled to a basic standard deduction of $4,400 under §162(c)(2)(B). Bob is age 27 in 2015 (born Feb 7, 1988), so he is not entitled to any additional standard deduction for being aged or blind.

4. **Taxable Income Calculation**:
   - Gross income: $43,591 (given fact)
   - Less: Standard deduction: $4,400 (§162(c)(2)(B))
   - Less: Personal exemptions: $4,000 (§151)

5. **Tax Calculation**: Under §1(b)(ii), for Head of Household with taxable income over $29,600 but not over $76,400, the tax is $4,440 plus 28% of the excess over $29,600.
   - Excess over $29,600 = $35,191 - $29,600 = $5,591
   - Tax = $4,440 + 0.28 × $5,591 = $4,440 + $1,565.48 = $6,005.48
