## Statute

Section 68(a) (lines 475-482):
"In the case of an individual whose adjusted gross income exceeds the applicable amount, the amount of the itemized deductions otherwise allowable for the taxable year shall be reduced by the lesser of-

    (1) 3 percent of the excess of adjusted gross income over the applicable amount, or

    (2) 80 percent of the amount of the itemized deductions otherwise allowable for such taxable year."

Section 68(b)(1)(B) (lines 489-491):
"For purposes of this section, the term 'applicable amount' means-

        (B) $275,000 in the case of a head of household (as defined in section 2(b)),"

## Reasoning

Alice's facts:
- Taxable year: 2016 (before Dec 31, 2017, so Section 68 applies per Section 68(f))
- Filing status: Head of household
- Adjusted gross income: $277,192
- Itemized deductions: $60,000

Step 1: Determine applicable amount
From Section 68(b)(1)(B), for head of household, applicable amount = $275,000

Step 2: Calculate excess of AGI over applicable amount
$277,192 - $275,000 = $2,192

Step 3: Calculate reduction under Section 68(a)
Per Section 68(a), reduction is the lesser of:
- Option (1): 3 percent of excess = 0.03 × $2,192 = $65.76
- Option (2): 80 percent of itemized deductions = 0.80 × $60,000 = $48,000

>>> excess = 277192 - 275000  # = 2192

The actual reduction is $65.76 (the lesser of $65.76 and $48,000).

The claim states the reduction is $48,000, but the statute requires the lesser of the two amounts, which is $65.76.
