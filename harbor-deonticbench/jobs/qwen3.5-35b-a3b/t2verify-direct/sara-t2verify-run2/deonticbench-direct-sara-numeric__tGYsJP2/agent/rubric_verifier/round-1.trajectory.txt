2. **Gross income**: $103,272 (given in case facts).

4. **Personal exemptions**: Under §151(d)(1), the exemption amount is $2,000. Alice is entitled to her own exemption of $2,000. Under §151(b), she is entitled to an additional exemption for her spouse because: (a) they file a joint return, (b) the spouse has no gross income, and (c) the spouse is not a dependent of another taxpayer. Therefore, personal exemptions = $2,000 + $2,000 = $4,000.

6. **Taxable income calculation**:
   - Gross income: $103,272
   - Less standard deduction: $6,000
   - Less personal exemptions: $4,000
