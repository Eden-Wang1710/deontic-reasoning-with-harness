2. **Gross income**: $103,272 (given in case facts).

4. **Personal exemptions**: Under §151(d)(1), the exemption amount is $2,000. Alice is entitled to her own exemption of $2,000. 6. **Taxable income calculation**:
   - Gross income: $103,272
