## Reasoning

1. **Filing Status**: Alice is unmarried (no mention of spouse). She is not a surviving spouse (her father Bob is alive) and not a head of household (no qualifying children). Therefore, Alice files as an unmarried individual under §1(c).

2. **Standard Deduction**: Under §63(c)(2)(C), the basic standard deduction for "any other case" (unmarried individuals) is $3,000.

3. **Personal Exemptions**:
   - Alice gets $2,000 for herself under §151(b).
   - Bob is Alice's father (relationship under §152(d)(2)(C)). Bob has no income in 2015 (§152(d)(1)(B)). Bob is not Alice's qualifying child (he is her father, not her child) and not a qualifying child of anyone else. Therefore, Bob qualifies as Alice's dependent under §152(d) as a qualifying relative. Alice gets $2,000 for Bob under §151(c).
   - Total exemptions before phaseout: $2,000 + $2,000 = $4,000.

4. **Exemption Phaseout** (§151(d)(3) and §68(b)(1)(C)):
   - Alice's AGI = $264,215 (given as gross income, no adjustments mentioned).
   - Applicable amount for unmarried individuals under §68(b)(1)(C) = $250,000.
   - Excess AGI = $264,215 - $250,000 = $14,215.
   - Applicable percentage = 2 percentage points × number of $2,500 fractions.
   - Number of fractions = ceil($14,215 / $2,500) = ceil(5.686) = 6.
   - Applicable percentage = 6 × 2% = 12%.
   - Reduced exemptions = $4,000 × (1 - 0.12) = $4,000 × 0.88 = $3,520.

5. **Taxable Income** (§63(b)):
   - Taxable income = AGI - standard deduction - reduced exemptions
   - Taxable income = $264,215 - $3,000 - $3,520 = $257,695.

6. **Tax Calculation** (§1(c)(v)):
   - Alice's taxable income ($257,695) exceeds $250,000.
   - Tax = $79,772 + 39.6% × ($257,695 - $250,000)
   - Tax = $79,772 + 0.396 × $7,695
   - Tax = $79,772 + $3,047.22
   - Tax = $82,819.22
   - Rounded to whole number: $82,819
