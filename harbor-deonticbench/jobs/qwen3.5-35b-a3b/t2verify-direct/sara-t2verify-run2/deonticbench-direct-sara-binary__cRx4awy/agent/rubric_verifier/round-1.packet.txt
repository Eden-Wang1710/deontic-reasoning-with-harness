"(1) agricultural labor unless-

    (A) such labor is performed for a person who-

        (i) during the calendar year or the preceding calendar year paid remuneration in cash of $20,000 or more to individuals employed in agricultural labor (including labor performed by an alien referred to in subparagraph (B)), or

        (ii) on each of some 10 days during the calendar year or during the preceding calendar year, each day being in a different calendar week, employed in agricultural labor (including labor performed by an alien referred to in subparagraph (B)) for some portion of the day (whether or not at the same moment of time) 5 or more individuals; and

    (B) such labor is not agricultural labor performed by an individual who is an alien admitted to the United States to perform agricultural labor pursuant to sections 214(c) and 101(a)(15)(H) of the Immigration and Nationality Act."

3. The threshold for agricultural labor (Section 3306(c)(1)(A)(i)): "during the calendar year or the preceding calendar year paid remuneration in cash of $20,000 or more to individuals employed in agricultural labor"

6. Therefore, Section 3306(c)(1) applies to Alice employing Bob for the year 2017.
