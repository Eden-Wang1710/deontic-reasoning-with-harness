## Statute

Section 3306(b)(10) (lines 295-301):

"(10) any payment or series of payments by an employer to an employee or any of his dependents which is paid-

    (A) upon or after the termination of an employee's employment relationship because of (i) death, or (ii) retirement for disability, and

    (B) under a plan established by the employer which makes provision for his employees generally or a class or classes of his employees (or for such employees or class or classes of employees and their dependents),

    other than any such payment or series of payments which would have been paid if the employee's employment relationship had not been so terminated;"

## Reasoning

The claim states that Section 3306(b)(10)(B) applies to the payment of $12,980 that Alice made in 2019.

Section 3306(b)(10) excludes from "wages" any payment made:
- (A) upon or after termination due to death or retirement for disability, AND
- (B) under a plan established by the employer which makes provision for employees generally

Applying the facts:
- Bob was diagnosed as disabled and retired on Oct 10, 2019 (case fact)
- Alice paid Bob $12,980 upon termination (case fact: $12980)
- Alice had no plan established to provide for her employees' disabilities (case fact)

For Section 3306(b)(10)(B) to apply, condition (B) must be met. However, the facts state that "Alice had no plan established to provide for her employees' disabilities." Therefore, condition (B) is NOT satisfied.

Since Section 3306(b)(10)(B) requires the payment to be made "under a plan established by the employer" and Alice had no such plan, Section 3306(b)(10)(B) does NOT apply to this payment.

The claim that Section 3306(b)(10)(B) applies is therefore FALSE.

## Answer
Answer: Contradiction
