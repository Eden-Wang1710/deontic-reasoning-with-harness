Alice furnishes over one-half the cost of maintaining the household (she maintains the home). Bob (her spouse) has a different principal place of abode than Alice and Charlie in 2019, meaning Bob is not a member of Alice's household for the last 6 months of 2019. Under §7703(b), Alice "shall not be considered as married."

2. **Head of Household Status (§2(b)(1))**: Since Alice is not considered married at the close of her taxable year, maintains a household that constitutes for more than one-half of the taxable year the principal place of abode of her qualifying child Charlie, and is entitled to a deduction for Charlie under §151, Alice qualifies as a head of household.

4. **Personal Exemptions (§151)**: Alice is entitled to:

   - One exemption for dependent Charlie: $2,000 (§151(c) and (d)(1))
   Total exemptions: $4,000
