## Statute

Section 152(c)(1) - Qualifying child:
"(1) In general

    The term 'qualifying child' means, with respect to any taxpayer for any taxable year, an individual-

        (A) who bears a relationship to the taxpayer described in paragraph (2),

        (B) who has the same principal place of abode as the taxpayer for more than one-half of such taxable year,

        (C) who meets the age requirements of paragraph (3), and

        (E) who has not filed a joint return (other than only for a claim of refund) with the individual's spouse for the taxable year beginning in the calendar year in which the taxable year of the taxpayer begins."

Section 152(c)(2) - Relationship:
"(2) Relationship

    For purposes of paragraph (1)(A), an individual bears a relationship to the taxpayer described in this paragraph if such individual is-

        (A) a child of the taxpayer or a descendant of such a child, or

        (B) a brother, sister, stepbrother, or stepsister of the taxpayer or a descendant of any such relative."

## Reasoning

Applying Section 152(c)(1) to the case facts:

1. **Relationship (152(c)(1)(A) and 152(c)(2)(A))**: Bob is Alice's son, which satisfies the relationship requirement.

2. **Residency (152(c)(1)(B))**: The facts state Alice and Bob lived in the same home from September 1st, 2015 to November 3rd, 2019. For 2019, Bob lived with Alice from January 1, 2019 through November 3, 2019.

   Days calculation:
   ```
   Days in 2019 = 365 (not a leap year)
   Half of 2019 = 365/2 = 182.5 days required
   Days Bob lived with Alice in 2019 = 307 days
   ```

   Since 307 > 182.5, the residency requirement is met.

3. **Age (152(c)(1)(C))**: The facts state "Bob satisfied section 152(c)(2) and 152(c)(3) with Alice as the taxpayer for the years 2015 to 2020." This confirms Bob met the age requirements for 2019.

4. **Joint return (152(c)(1)(E))**: The facts do not state that Bob filed a joint return with Charlie for 2019. Bob married Charlie on October 23rd, 2018, but there is no indication of a joint return filing for 2019. Therefore, this requirement is satisfied.

All four requirements of Section 152(c)(1) are met. Bob is a qualifying child of Alice for the year 2019.
