## Statute

Section 2(b)(iii) - Head of household tax rates:
"(iii) $17,544, plus 31% of the excess over $76,400 if the taxable income is over $76,400 but not over $127,500;"

Section 2(b)(1)(B) - Head of household definition:
"(B) maintains a household which constitutes for such taxable year the principal place of abode of the father or mother of the taxpayer, if the taxpayer is entitled to a deduction for the taxable year for such father or mother under section 151."

Section 394(B) - Basic standard deduction:
"(B) $4,400 in the case of a head of household (as defined in section 2(b)), or"

Section 151(d)(1) - Exemption amount:
"Except as otherwise provided in this subsection, the term 'exemption amount' means $2,000."

Section 151(b) - Taxpayer and spouse exemption:
"An exemption of the exemption amount for the taxpayer; and an additional exemption of the exemption amount for the spouse of the taxpayer if a joint return is not made by the taxpayer and his spouse, and if the spouse, for the calendar year in which the taxable year of the taxpayer begins, has no gross income and is not the dependent of another taxpayer."

Section 152(d)(1)(B) - Qualifying relative income requirement:
"(B) who has no income for the calendar year in which such taxable year begins, and"

Section 152(d)(2)(C) - Qualifying relative relationship:
"(C) The father or mother, or an ancestor of either."

## Reasoning

1. Alice's gross income for 2015 is $102,268 (given in case facts).

2. Alice qualifies as head of household under section 2(b)(1)(B) because:
   - She is not married (no spouse mentioned in facts)
   - Bob (her father) has his principal place of abode in her household (case facts)
   - Bob has no income (case facts), so Alice is entitled to a deduction for him under section 151
   - Section 152(d)(2)(C) confirms a father qualifies as a qualifying relative

3. Alice's standard deduction is $4,400 under section 394(B) for head of household.

4. Alice's exemptions total $4,000:
   - $2,000 for herself (section 151(d)(1))
   - $2,000 for Bob (section 151(b))

5. Taxable income = $102,268 - $4,400 - $4,000 = $93,868

6. Under section 2(b)(iii), since taxable income ($93,868) is over $76,400 but not over $127,500:
   Tax = $17,544 + 31% × ($93,868 - $76,400)
   Tax = $17,544 + 0.31 × $17,468
   Tax = $17,544 + $5,415.08
   Tax = $22,959.08

7. Rounded to whole dollar: $22,959
