Bob qualifies as a qualifying relative under §152(d) because he is Alice's father (§152(d)(2)(C)), has no income (§152(d)(1)(B)), and is not a qualifying child
(§152(d)(1)(D)).

As head of household, Alice's standard deduction is $4,400 under §63(c)(2)(B).

Alice's AGI exceeds the $250,000 threshold under §68(b)(1)(C), triggering exemption phaseout under §151(d)(3). The applicable percentage is 50% (25 increments o
f $2,500 over threshold), reducing the $2,000 exemption amount to $1,000 per exemption. With two exemptions (Alice and Bob), total exemptions are $2,000.

Taxable income = $311,510 - $4,400 - $2,000 = $305,110.
