§151(d)(1) - Exemption amount

## Reasoning
Alice's gross income consists of:
- $200 for services performed at Johns Hopkins Hospital
- $31,220 in remuneration
Total gross income = $31,420

Alice is an unmarried individual (not married, not head of household, not surviving spouse).

Standard deduction (§63(c)(2)(C)): $3,000 for unmarried individuals
Personal exemption (§151(d)(1)): $2,000

Taxable income = Gross income - Standard deduction - Personal exemption
Taxable income = $31,420 - $3,000 - $2,000 = $26,420

Under §1(c)(ii), for unmarried individuals with taxable income over $22,100 but not over $53,500:
Tax = $3,315 + 28% of excess over $22,100
Excess = $26,420 - $22,100 = $4,320
28% of $4,320 = $1,209.60
Tax = $3,315 + $1,209.60 = $4,524.60

Rounded to whole dollar: $4,525
