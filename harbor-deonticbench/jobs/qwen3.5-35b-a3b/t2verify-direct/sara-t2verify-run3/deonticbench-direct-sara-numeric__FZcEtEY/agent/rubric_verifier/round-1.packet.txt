## Reasoning

Alice maintains a household for her son Charlie (born 2012, under 19) for more than half the year and furnishes over half the cost. Charlie has no gross income and is not a dependent of another taxpayer. Alice qualifies as head of household under §2(b) and as the parent claiming Charlie as a dependent under §152.

Alice's income calculation:
- Gross income: $9,560
- Standard deduction (head of household per §63(c)(2)(B)): $4,400
- Personal exemptions: $4,000 ($2,000 for Alice under §151(b) + $2,000 for Charlie under §151(c))
- Taxable income: $9,560 - $4,400 - $4,000 = $1,160

Under §1(b), head of households with taxable income not over $29,600 pay 15% of taxable income.

Tax: $1,160 × 15% = $174
