## Reasoning
Alice was legally separated under a decree of divorce on March 2, 2015, so under §7703(a)(2) she is not considered married in 2017.

Under §2(b), Alice qualifies as head of household because:
- She is not married at the close of 2017 (divorced 2015)
- She is not a surviving spouse
- She maintains a household that is Charlie's principal place of abode for more than half the year
- She furnishes over half the cost of maintaining the household

Charlie qualifies as Alice's dependent under §152(c) as a qualifying child:
- Relationship: Charlie is Alice's child
- Same principal place of abode for more than half the year (they live together)
- Age requirement: Charlie was born Feb 2, 2012, so he was 5 in 2017 (under 25)
- Not filing a joint return (a 5-year-old cannot file)

Alice's income calculation:
- Gross income: $9,560
- Standard deduction (head of household per §63(c)(2)(B)): $4,400
- Personal exemptions: $4,000 ($2,000 for Alice under §151(b) + $2,000 for Charlie under §151(c))
- Taxable income: $9,560 - $4,400 - $4,000 = $1,160

Under §1(b), head of households with taxable income not over $29,600 pay 15% of taxable income.

Tax: $1,160 × 15% = $174
