## Reasoning
Section 152(d)(2)(H) defines a qualifying relationship where an individual bears a relationship to the taxpayer if the individual:
1. Is NOT the spouse of the taxpayer (determined without regard to section 7703)
2. Has the same principal place of abode as the taxpayer for the taxable year
3. Is a member of the taxpayer's household

The facts state that Alice and Bob started living together on April 15th, 2014, and are not married. For the 2018 taxable year:

- Alice has the same principal place of abode as Bob (they have lived together since 2014) - condition 2 satisfied
- Alice is a member of Bob's household (they live together) - condition 3 satisfied
