## Statute
§3306(a)(1)(B)

## Reasoning
Section 3306(a)(1)(B) defines an "employer" as a person who, on each of some 10 days during the calendar year (or preceding calendar year), each day being in a different calendar week, employed at least one individual in employment for some portion of the day.

Alice employed Bob on 12 dates in 2017: Jan 24, Feb 4, Mar 3, Mar 18, Apr 1, May 9, Oct 14, Oct 25, Nov 8, Nov 22, Dec 1, and Dec 2.

Checking calendar weeks (ISO week numbering):
- Jan 24, 2017 (Tuesday) = Week 4
- Feb 4, 2017 (Saturday) = Week 5
- Mar 3, 2017 (Friday) = Week 9
- Mar 18, 2017 (Saturday) = Week 11
- Apr 1, 2017 (Saturday) = Week 13
- May 9, 2017 (Tuesday) = Week 19
- Oct 14, 2017 (Saturday) = Week 41
- Oct 25, 2017 (Wednesday) = Week 43
- Nov 8, 2017 (Wednesday) = Week 45
- Nov 22, 2017 (Wednesday) = Week 47
- Dec 1, 2017 (Friday) = Week 48
- Dec 2, 2017 (Saturday) = Week 48

Dec 1 and Dec 2 fall in the same calendar week (week 48). This leaves 11 different calendar weeks. Since 11 >= 10, Alice satisfies the requirement of §3306(a)(1)(B). Therefore, Section 3306(a)(1)(B) applies to Alice for the year 2017.
