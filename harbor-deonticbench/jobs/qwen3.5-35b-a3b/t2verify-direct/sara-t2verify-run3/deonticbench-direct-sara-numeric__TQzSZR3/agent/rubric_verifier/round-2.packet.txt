2. Standard Deduction: Under §63(c)(2)(A)(i), the basic standard deduction for a joint return is 200% of the amount in §63(c)(2)(C). Since §63(c)(2)(C) sets the base amount at $3,000, the standard deduction for a joint return is 200% × $3,000 = $6,000.

3. Personal Exemptions: Under §151, they are entitled to:
   - Taxpayer exemption for Alice: $2,000 (§151(a))
   - Spouse exemption for Bob: $2,000 (joint return filing includes both spouses)
   - Dependent exemption for Charlie: $2,000 (§151(c))
   
   Charlie qualifies as a dependent under §152(c) as a qualifying child: (A) he is their child, (B) he lives with them, (C) he is under 25 (born 2000, so age 12-13 in 2013), and (E) he has not filed a joint return.
   
   Total exemptions: $6,000 (§151(d)(1))
   The phaseout under §151(d)(3) does not apply because their AGI of $121,800 is below the applicable amount.

4. Taxable Income: $121,800 - $6,000 (standard deduction) - $6,000 (exemptions) = $109,800

5. Tax Calculation: Under §1(a)(iii), for taxable income over $89,150 but not over $140,000:
   Tax = $20,165 + 31% × ($109,800 - $89,150)
   Tax = $20,165 + 31% × $20,650
   Tax = $20,165 + $6,401.50
   Tax = $26,566.50
