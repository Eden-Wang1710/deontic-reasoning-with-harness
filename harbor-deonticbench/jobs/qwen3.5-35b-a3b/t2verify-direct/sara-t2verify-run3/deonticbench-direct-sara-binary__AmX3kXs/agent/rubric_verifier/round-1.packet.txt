## Statute
§152(c)(2)(A)

## Reasoning
Section 152(c)(2) defines when an individual bears a relationship to a taxpayer for purposes of being a qualifying child. Under §152(c)(2)(A), an individual bears a relationship to the taxpayer if such individual is "a child of the taxpayer or a descendant of such a child."

The case facts state that Alice has a son Bob. This means Bob is the child of Alice (the taxpayer), not the other way around.

The claim states "Alice bears a relationship to Bob under section 152(c)(2)." However, under the statute, Bob (the child) bears a relationship to Alice (the taxpayer), not Alice bearing a relationship to Bob. For Alice to bear a relationship to Bob under §152(c)(2), Alice would need to be Bob's child or descendant, which she is not.

The direction of the relationship in the claim is reversed from what the statute defines.

## Answer
Answer: Contradiction
