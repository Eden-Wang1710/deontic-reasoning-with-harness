## Reasoning
Section 152(c)(2) defines which individuals bear a relationship to a taxpayer for qualifying child purposes. Paragraph (A) specifically covers "a child of the taxpayer or a descendant of such a child." Paragraph (B) covers "a brother, sister, stepbrother, or stepsister of the taxpayer or a descendant of any such relative."

Bob is Alice's brother. This relationship falls under §152(c)(2)(B), not §152(c)(2)(A). The claim that Bob bears a relationship to Alice under §152(c)(2)(A) specifically is false because brothers are covered under subsection (B), not subsection (A).
