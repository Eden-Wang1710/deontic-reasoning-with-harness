## Statute
§152(b)(1) - Dependents ineligible

## Reasoning
Section 152(b)(1) states: "If an individual is a dependent of a taxpayer for any taxable year of such taxpayer beginning in a calendar year, such individual shall be treated as having no dependents for any taxable year of such individual beginning in such calendar year."

This provision applies to an individual who IS a dependent of another taxpayer. It disallows that dependent from having their own dependents.

Case facts:
- Alice has a son Bob who satisfies 152(c)(1) for 2015 (Bob is Alice's qualifying child/dependent)
- Bob has a son Charlie who satisfies 152(c)(1) for 2015 (Charlie is Bob's qualifying child)
- Alice's income: $504,598; Bob's income: $0

For §152(b)(1) to apply to Alice, Alice would need to be a dependent of someone else. However:
- Alice is Bob's mother and taxpayer, not his dependent
- Bob is Alice's dependent (qualifying child)
- Charlie is Bob's dependent

§152(b)(1) applies to Bob (not Alice) because Bob is a dependent of Alice. Bob is treated as having no dependents for 2015.

The claim that "Section 152(b)(1) applies to Alice for the year 2015" is false - it contradicts the facts because Alice is not a dependent of any taxpayer.

## Answer
Answer: Contradiction
