## Reasoning
Section 2(b)(1)(A)(ii) states that an individual shall be considered a head of household if they are not married at the close of their taxable year, not a surviving spouse, and maintain a household for 'any other person who is a dependent of the taxpayer, if the taxpayer is entitled to a deduction for the taxable year for such person under section 151.'

Applying to the facts:
1. Bob was not married at the close of 2017 because Alice died on July 9, 2014 (section 2(b)(2)(C) only considers a taxpayer married if spouse died during the taxable year).
2. Bob was not a surviving spouse because Alice died in 2014, not 2017 (section 2(a) requires spouse to die during the taxable year).
3. Bob maintained a household for Charlie from 2004 to 2019, including 2017, and furnished the costs of maintaining the home.
4. Charlie was a dependent of Bob in 2017, as explicitly stated: 'From 2015 to 2019, Bob was entitled to a deduction for Charlie under section 151(c).'
5. Bob was entitled to a deduction for Charlie under section 151 in 2017.

All conditions of §2(b)(1)(A)(ii) are satisfied, so the claim that this provision applies to Charlie as the dependent in 2017 is true.
