## Reasoning
Section 2(b)(1)(A)(ii) states that an individual shall be considered a head of household if they are not married at the close of their taxable year, not a surviving spouse, and maintain a household for 'any other person who is a dependent of the taxpayer, if the taxpayer is entitled to a deduction for the taxable year for such person under section 151.'

Section 151(c) provides: 'An exemption of the exemption amount for each individual who is a dependent (as defined in section 152) of the taxpayer for the taxable year.'

Section 152(c) defines 'qualifying child' requirements:
- (A) Relationship: Charlie is Bob's brother (152(c)(2)(B))
- (B) Same principal place of abode: Bob furnished the home where Charlie lived from 2004-2019
- (C) Age: Charlie born Oct 9, 2000 was 17 at end of 2017 (younger than Bob and under 25)

Applying to the facts:
1. Bob was not married at close of 2017 (Alice died July 9, 2014; §2(b)(2)(C) only considers taxpayer married if spouse died during taxable year)
2. Bob was not a surviving spouse (Alice died in 2014, not 2017)
3. Bob maintained a household for Charlie in 2017 and furnished over half the costs
4. Charlie was a dependent of Bob in 2017, as facts state: 'From 2015 to 2019, Bob was entitled to a deduction for Charlie under section 151(c)'

All conditions of §2(b)(1)(A)(ii) are satisfied. The claim that this provision applies to Charlie as the dependent in 2017 is true.
