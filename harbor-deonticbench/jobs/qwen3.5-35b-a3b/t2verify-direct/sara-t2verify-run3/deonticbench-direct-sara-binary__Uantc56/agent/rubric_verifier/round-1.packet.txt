## Statute
§3306(a)(2)(B)

## Reasoning
Section 3306(a)(2)(B) defines an employer for agricultural labor as someone who, "on each of some 10 days during the calendar year or during the preceding calendar year, each day being in a different calendar week, employed at least 5 individuals in employment in agricultural labor."

Analyzing Alice's employment of agricultural workers in 2017:
- Jan 24: 5 workers (Bob, Cameron, Dan, Emily, Fred) - Week 4
- Feb 4: 3 workers - does not qualify
- Mar 3: 5 workers (Bob, Cameron, Dan, Emily, Fred) - Week 9
- Mar 18: 5 workers (Cameron, Dan, Emily, Fred, George) - Week 11
- Apr 1: 5 workers (Bob, Cameron, Dan, Fred, George) - Week 13
- May 9: 5 workers (Cameron, Dan, Emily, Fred, George) - Week 19
- Oct 14: 5 workers (Bob, Cameron, Dan, Emily, George) - Week 42
- Oct 25: 4 workers - does not qualify
- Nov 8: 5 workers (Bob, Cameron, Emily, Fred, George) - Week 45
- Nov 22: 5 workers (Bob, Cameron, Dan, Emily, Fred) - Week 47
- Dec 1: 5 workers (Bob, Cameron, Dan, Emily, George) - Week 48
- Dec 2: 5 workers (Bob, Cameron, Dan, Emily, George) - Week 48

Dates with 5+ workers: 10 dates (Jan 24, Mar 3, Mar 18, Apr 1, May 9, Oct 14, Nov 8, Nov 22, Dec 1, Dec 2)

However, Dec 1 and Dec 2 both fall in the same calendar week (Week 48, December 25-31). Therefore, these 10 dates span only 9 different calendar weeks.

The statute requires 10 days, each in a different calendar week. Alice employed at least 5 workers on only 9 different calendar weeks, not 10.

Therefore, Section 3306(a)(2)(B) does NOT apply to Alice.

## Answer
Answer: Contradiction
