§3306(c)(2)

## Reasoning
Section 3306(c)(2) provides that domestic service in a private home is excluded from the definition of 'employment' UNLESS the person paid cash remuneration of $1,000 or more to individuals employed in such domestic service in the calendar year or preceding calendar year.

In this case, Alice paid wages of $3200 to Bob for domestic service done in her home from Feb 1st, 2017 to Sep 2nd, 2017. Since $3200 exceeds the $1,000 threshold specified in §3306(c)(2), the exception does not apply, meaning this domestic service is considered 'employment' under the statute.

Therefore, Section 3306(c)(2) applies to Alice employing Bob for the year 2017.
