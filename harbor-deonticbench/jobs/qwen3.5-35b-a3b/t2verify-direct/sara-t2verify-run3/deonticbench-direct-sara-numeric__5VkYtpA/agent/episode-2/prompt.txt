## Statute
§1(b) - Tax rates for heads of households
§63(c)(2)(B) - Standard deduction for head of household ($9,250)
§151(b) - Exemption for taxpayer
§151(c) - Exemption for dependents
§151(d)(1) - Exemption amount ($2,000)

## Reasoning
Alice files as head of household because she is unmarried and maintains a household for her dependent brother Bob (who qualifies as a dependent under §152 as a
qualifying relative).

Bob is Alice's dependent because he is her brother (relationship test), has no gross income (gross income test), and Alice provides his support (support test).

Alice's taxable income = Gross income - Standard deduction - Personal exemptions
= $260,932 - $9,250 - $4,000 ($2,000 for Alice + $2,000 for Bob)
= $247,682

Since Alice's taxable income ($247,682) is over $127,500 but not over $250,000, she falls into the 36% bracket under §1(b)(iv).

Tax = $33,385 + 36% × ($247,682 - $127,500)
    = $33,385 + 36% × $120,182
    = $33,385 + $43,265.52
    = $76,650.52
    = $76,651 (rounded to whole dollar)
