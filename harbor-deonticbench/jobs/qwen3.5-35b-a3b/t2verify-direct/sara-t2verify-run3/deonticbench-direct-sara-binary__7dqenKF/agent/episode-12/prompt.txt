## Statute
§151(b) - Taxpayer and spouse
§151(d)(1) - Exemption amount
§7703(a) - Determination of marital status

## Reasoning
Under §151(b), an additional exemption for a spouse requires three conditions:
1. A joint return is not made by the taxpayer and spouse
2. The spouse has no gross income for the calendar year
3. The spouse is not the dependent of another taxpayer

The facts affirmatively establish:
- Alice and Bob are married (married since Feb 2, 2015)
- Bob has no gross income for 2015

The facts do NOT affirmatively establish:
- Whether a joint return was made or not
- Whether Bob is claimed as a dependent by another taxpayer

The claim "Alice can receive an exemption for Bob under section 151(b)" requires all three conditions to be satisfied. Since the facts do not affirmatively esta
blish conditions 1 and 3, the claim is not supported by the facts. The facts are insufficient to conclude that Alice can receive the exemption.

## Answer
Answer: Contradiction
