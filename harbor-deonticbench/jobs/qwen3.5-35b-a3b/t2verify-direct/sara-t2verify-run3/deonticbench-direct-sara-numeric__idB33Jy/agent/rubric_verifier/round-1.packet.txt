## Reasoning

Gross income: $133,200
Itemized deductions: $2,939 + $8,744 = $11,683
Personal exemption (single, no dependents): $2,000

Taxable income = $133,200 - $11,683 - $2,000 = $119,517

Since $119,517 is over $115,000 but not over $250,000, under §1(c)(iv):
Tax = $31,172 + 36% × ($119,517 - $115,000)
Tax = $31,172 + 36% × $4,517
Tax = $31,172 + $1,626.12
Tax = $32,798.12

Rounded to whole number: $32,798
