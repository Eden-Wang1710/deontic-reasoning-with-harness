## Reasoning
Section 152(d)(1)(D) is part of the definition of a qualifying relative. It requires that an individual be "not a qualifying child of such taxpayer or of any other taxpayer for any taxable year beginning in the calendar year in which such taxable year begins."

For section 152(d)(1)(D) to apply to Bob, Bob must not be a qualifying child of Alice or any other taxpayer for 2015.

Under §152(c)(3), a qualifying child must be "younger than the taxpayer claiming such individual as a qualifying child and is less than 25 years old at the end of the taxable year."

Since Bob is Alice's father, Bob is older than Alice. Therefore, Bob cannot be Alice's qualifying child because he fails the age requirement (must be younger than the taxpayer).

Consequently, section 152(d)(1)(D) applies to Bob for 2015 because Bob is not a qualifying child of Alice or any other taxpayer.
