§2(a)(2)(B)

## Reasoning
Section 2(a)(2)(B) states that a taxpayer shall not be considered a surviving spouse unless, for the taxable year during which the spouse died, a joint return could have been made. It further provides that no joint return shall be made if either spouse at any time during the taxable year is a nonresident alien.

Facts:
- Alice died on July 9th, 2014
- Alice was a nonresident alien since March 4th, 1990

Since Alice was a nonresident alien at some point during the 2014 taxable year (she died on July 9, 2014, and had been a nonresident alien since 1990), Bob and Alice could NOT have made a joint return in 2014. Therefore, section 2(a)(2)(B) applies to Bob in 2014 - it operates as a limitation preventing him from being considered a surviving spouse.

Answer: Entailment
