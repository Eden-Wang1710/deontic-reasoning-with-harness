## Reasoning
Alice is unmarried. She maintains a household with her father Bob since 2012.

**Head of Household Status (§2(b))**:
- Alice is not married at close of taxable year (unmarried)
- Bob has the same principal place of abode as Alice (same household since 2012)
- Bob is Alice's father - qualifies under §2(b)(1)(B)
- Bob has NO income since 2012, so he cannot furnish over half the household cost; therefore Alice furnishes over half
- Bob qualifies as Alice's dependent under §152(d) (qualifying relative - father with no income)
- Alice is entitled to deduction for Bob under §151(c)

**Deductions**:
- Standard deduction: $4,400 (§63(c)(2)(B) - head of household)
- Personal exemption for Alice: $2,000 (§151(b), §151(d)(1))
- Personal exemption for Bob (dependent): $2,000 (§151(c), §151(d)(1))
- Total deductions: $8,400

**Taxable Income**:
$102,268 - $8,400 = $93,868

**Tax Calculation (§1(b)(iii))**:
For head of household with taxable income over $76,400 but not over $127,500:
Tax = $17,544 + 31% × ($93,868 - $76,400)
Tax = $17,544 + 31% × $17,468
Tax = $17,544 + $5,415.08 = $22,959.08

Rounding to whole dollar: $22,959
