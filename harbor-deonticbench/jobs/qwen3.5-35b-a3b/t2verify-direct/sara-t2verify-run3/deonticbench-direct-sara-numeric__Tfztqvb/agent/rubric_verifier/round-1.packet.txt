## Reasoning
Alice is an unmarried individual (not married, not a surviving spouse, not head of household). Her gross income is $311,510.

Under §63(c)(2)(A), Alice's standard deduction is $3,000.

Under §151(d)(1), Alice's personal exemption is $2,000, but this is subject to the phaseout under §151(d)(3).

Under §68(b)(3)(C), the applicable amount for unmarried individuals is $250,000. Alice's AGI of $311,510 exceeds this by $61,510.

Under §151(d)(3)(B), the applicable percentage is 1% + 0.2% for each $2,500 (or fraction thereof) by which AGI exceeds the applicable amount.

Excess = $61,510. Number of $2,500 units = $61,510 / $2,500 = 24.604, which rounds up to 25 (fraction thereof).

Applicable percentage = 1% + 0.2% × 25 = 1% + 5% = 6%.

Thus, Alice's personal exemption is reduced by 6%: $2,000 × (1 - 0.06) = $1,880.

Taxable income = $311,510 - $3,000 - $1,880 = $306,630.

Under §1(c)(v), for taxable income over $250,000, tax = $79,772 + 39.6% of excess over $250,000.

Tax = $79,772 + 0.396 × ($306,630 - $250,000)
    = $79,772 + 0.396 × $56,630
    = $79,772 + $22,425.48
    = $102,197.48

Rounded to the nearest dollar: $102,197
