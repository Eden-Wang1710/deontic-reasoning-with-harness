## Statute
§2(b)(1)(B) - requires maintaining household of taxpayer's father or mother

## Reasoning
Section 2(b)(1)(B) states that an individual shall be considered a head of household if they maintain a household which constitutes for the taxable year the principal place of abode of the **father or mother** of the taxpayer, if the taxpayer is entitled to a deduction for such father or mother under section 151.

The facts state that Charlie is Bob's **child** (born October 9th, 2000), and Bob is entitled to a deduction for Charlie under section 151(c) for 2015-2019.

Section 2(b)(1)(B) specifically requires maintaining a parent (father or mother), not a child. Charlie is Bob's child, not his father or mother. Therefore, §2(b)(1)(B) does not apply to Bob in 2018.

The claim that §2(b)(1)(B) applies to Bob in 2018 is false - it contradicts the statute's requirements.

## Answer
Answer: Contradiction
